# Code of conduct

RouteFoundry is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, nationality, disability, appearance, or
belief.

Be constructive, assume good intent while discussing evidence, respect privacy, and focus
criticism on ideas and reproducible behavior. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's
private data are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should be private,
proportionate, documented, and open to a good-faith appeal.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private maintainer contact or private
security reporting. Do not expose a reporter publicly. This policy applies in repository
spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
